# Three-minute walkthrough

Use the research and challenge workspaces with real, completed records. If only the practice workspace is available, say it is a software demonstration with generated tones and no completed study.

**0:00–0:25: The question.** "I wanted to measure my own listening: how accurately and how consistently I can tell real voices from modern AI clones, and which cues I actually rely on. The setup keeps every answer, and any model output, hidden until my labels are locked." Name what you actually reviewed and what was excluded.

**0:25–1:05: Blind review.** Open an unreviewed clip in Review. Play it, replay a short interval, and show the guide beside it. Enter interference tags, the cues you heard, a timestamped observation, and a judgment with confidence. Explain why "uncertain" is allowed and requires a reason. Show Save & skip, so unfinished work is never lost or rushed.

**1:05–1:40: Quality control.** In Quality Review, open a revealed disagreement or escalation. Show the original judgment, the blind repeat from 48 hours later or an independent reviewer's label, and any adjudication, each kept separately. State the self-agreement score you actually measured. If no second person reviewed, say "independent agreement was not measured."

**1:40–2:20: What the evidence showed.** Show the challenge results: which clones fooled you and whether the detector caught them. Then show "Which cues held up": the cues that matched the truth, and the ones that misled you. Open one written case and separate four things: the dataset reference, what you heard, what the detector said, and your possible explanation.

**2:20–3:00: Handoff.** Show the CSV export open in a spreadsheet, one SQL query, and the printed findings report. State counts with denominators, uncertainty, and limits. Name one guide change you would make next and why, and note that a fresh batch is needed to check it.
