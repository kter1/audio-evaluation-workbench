Scope, method, and attribution

The local cached speech model supplies transcription and estimated word times. It does not establish speaker identity, authenticity, intelligibility, emotion, or background-noise absence. Noise, reverb, music, overlap, and perceptual cues require a separate listening pass. No voice-origin detector was run. No audio was sent to an external API.
